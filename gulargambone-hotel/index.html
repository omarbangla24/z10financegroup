About Us

A Country Pub With a Long History

The Gulargambone Hotel has a long service history stretching back to 1930. Once a majestic two-storey grand hotel with a second-storey breezy veranda and accommodation, the hotel was raised to the ground by a fire in 1968, the day after Anzac Day.

Some time after the fire, the hotel was rebuilt on the same site at 18 Bourbah Street — right in the heart of the Gulargambone CBD — complete with accommodation, counter lunches, a beer garden and gaming room.

Our managers are community minded and hold lots of functions to support the local community. Complimentary camping is available on the adjacent block.
