About Us

Great Food, Smooth Coffee & Community Vibes

Located at 15 Victoria Ave, The Entrance NSW 2261, our café is loved by locals and visitors for its relaxed atmosphere, family-friendly seating and generous portions.

We offer a mix of indoor tables and sunny outdoor spots — perfect for catch-ups, quiet moments or weekend brunches. Our team is dedicated to providing tasty food, smooth coffee and a genuine community vibe every day we open.

Whether you're dining in or grabbing something on the go, there's always something delicious waiting.
