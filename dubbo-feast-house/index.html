About Us

The Diverse Flavours of the World, in the Heart of Dubbo

Welcome to Dubbo Feast House Restaurant & Bar, where we bring the diverse flavours of the world to the heart of Dubbo, NSW, Australia. Nestled at 248 Myall St, we are proud to be a part of this vibrant community, offering a unique dining experience that combines traditional recipes with a contemporary twist.

Our menu is crafted to cater to a variety of tastes, ensuring there's something for everyone. Whether you're craving a juicy Chicken and Cheese Burger or the rich and flavorful Paneer Tikka Masala, fuchka, chotpoti, jhalmuri or tehari — our chefs use the freshest ingredients to prepare each dish with care and passion.

  • Paneer Tikka Masala
  • Fuchka & Chotpoti

  • Traditional recipes with a contemporary twist
